Privacy Policy

What this private deployment processes, where information can go, and how to contact the operator.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

AI processing is not local-only. The initial Hermes configuration uses OpenAI through the OpenAI Codex provider. Prompts and relevant context can leave the operator’s computer. Do not assume zero retention, end-to-end secrecy from the operator, or automatic deletion.

1. Who is responsible?

The private operator is GitHub user susheel009, providing the Hermes Discord application for Hermes Cornerstone. Contact the administrator who gave you access using your existing private team communication channel. See support and operator contact for the public fallback. This policy covers this deployment, not independently operated Hermes installations.

2. Information the bot can process

Discord identifiers and routing information
User IDs, usernames, server, channel and thread IDs, message IDs, timestamps, roles or permissions where needed for access control, and bot interaction metadata.
Messages and conversation context
Prompts, slash commands, bot replies, and relevant conversation history. Where channel-history backfill is enabled, nearby messages from other people may be included as context when the bot is addressed. A mention-only response setting does not mean only the mentioned message can be processed.
Attachments and optional voice
Files, images, file metadata, and links submitted to the assistant. If voice features are enabled and used, audio and transcriptions may be processed. Voice is not a claim that the bot continuously records a server.
Task and operational information
Tool inputs and results, generated files, error and diagnostic logs, service-usage information, session records, and saved memories used for continuity. Enabled connected services may return additional data needed for a requested task.
Support requests
Contact details and request information you choose to provide. Public GitHub issues are visible to others; do not place private data in them.

3. Why the information is used

Information is used to check access, understand and answer requests, carry out authorized tool actions, deliver replies, maintain conversation continuity and memories, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and handle support, privacy, and deletion requests. The operator does not use this deployment to sell personal data, build advertising profiles, or train its own AI models on Discord API data.

4. Who can receive information?

  • Discord: Discord carries messages, attachments, commands, and replies under its own Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI: the initial model provider receives prompts, selected history, relevant attachments or their extracted content, and tool results needed for generation. Its handling is subject to the applicable account, product terms, settings, and privacy policy. This policy does not promise a particular provider retention period or a training opt-out that has not been verified for the operator’s account.
  • Enabled tool providers and destinations: web search, page retrieval, speech, hosting, GitHub, or other connected services may receive the task inputs needed when those features are used. A command to send a message, upload a file, or publish content sends that information to the destination you authorize. Ask the operator which optional providers are enabled before submitting sensitive material.
  • The operator and authorized administrators: people administering the computer or service can access stored conversations, files, and logs. Other server members can see content posted in channels they can access.
  • Legal requirements: information may be disclosed where required by law or to address a substantiated security incident, consistent with applicable law and Discord’s requirements.

The operator must review provider terms and settings before processing Discord data, including restrictions on AI-model training. This page does not itself configure those settings or authorize model training. Material changes to providers or data uses will be disclosed to authorized team members before the changed processing.

5. Storage and retention

Hermes can persist transcripts, session databases, logs, downloaded attachments, generated files, and memories on the operator-controlled computer. Persistent memory is enabled in the initial configuration inspected for this deployment. These records can survive restarts and new conversations. No fixed automatic expiry has been verified for this deployment. The operator must review stored data and remove it when it is no longer needed for the stated purposes, when a valid deletion request requires it, or when applicable law or Discord’s terms require it.

Any backups or exports made by the operator may also contain data and must be considered during deletion. Information held independently by Discord, AI providers, or destinations to which you requested publication is subject to those services’ own controls and obligations. See the deletion process and its limits.

6. Your choices and requests

You may stop using the bot, ask for access to be revoked, ask what data and providers are involved, and request access, correction, or deletion of your information. Where applicable law provides additional rights, including objection, restriction, portability, or complaints to a data-protection authority, those rights are not limited by this policy. The operator may ask for proportionate verification that you control the relevant Discord account; never provide a password, access token, or identity document in a public issue.

Use a private conversation with your Hermes Cornerstone administrator for personal requests. Starting a new session with /new or /reset is not an erasure request.

7. Security and location

Private bot access does not make an ordinary Discord channel confidential. Limit permissions and approved users, and do not send secrets. No system can guarantee perfect security. The operator is responsible for access controls, secure credentials, and appropriate storage safeguards; this page is not a security audit or certification.

Information is processed on the operator’s computer and by the external services used for a task, potentially in other countries. Contact the operator for the relevant hosting and provider details before sending data subject to location restrictions.

8. This information site

These static pages do not contain analytics, advertising, cookies set by the site’s code, tracking scripts, or data-entry forms. GitHub Pages hosts them and may collect technical visitor information under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The bot and any GitHub support issue are separate from simply reading these pages.

9. Age and updates

The assistant is not intended for anyone below Discord’s minimum age or the minimum age required by local law. If you believe it has received data from an ineligible child, contact the operator privately. Updates will be dated above; material changes will also be communicated through the existing Hermes Cornerstone team channel.
