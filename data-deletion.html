Data deletion

A new chat is not the same as erasure. Here is how to request deletion of information held by this deployment.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Contact the operator privately

Contact the Hermes Cornerstone administrator who granted you access, using the existing team Discord server or other established team communication channel. Ask for a private conversation with the operator, GitHub user susheel009. Do not send the request to the AI bot and assume it has automatically completed deletion.

If you cannot reach the administrator, open a minimal public contact request on GitHub. State only that you need a private privacy-contact route. Wait for that route before sharing identifying or sensitive details. A GitHub account is required to post an issue.

2. Include only what is needed

In the private conversation, identify the Discord account involved and the data or approximate date range you want deleted. You can request deletion of all identifiable data held by this deployment, not just one conversation. The operator may verify control of the account through an existing trusted channel. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, recovery codes, or identity documents.

3. What the operator must review

  • Stored conversation history and session records associated with your account.
  • Saved memories, cached attachments, generated files, and identifiable tool results.
  • Operational logs, exports, and any backups under the operator’s control.
  • Data sent to a service provider where the operator can request deletion or is otherwise required to assist.

The operator should acknowledge the request, explain any verification needed, handle it without undue delay and within applicable legal deadlines, and confirm what was deleted or why a limited exception applies. Data required by law may be retained only for that reason and for the required period. If removal from a backup cannot be immediate, the operator must explain the limitation and prevent ordinary reuse pending removal.

4. Important limits

/new and /reset start a new conversation; they do not automatically remove transcripts, logs, or memories. Deleting a Discord message, removing the bot, or revoking its permissions does not by itself delete records on the operator’s computer.

The operator cannot promise to erase another service’s independently controlled records, other people’s copies, or content already published to an external destination. You may also need to use that service’s deletion tools or contact its operator. The operator should explain what assistance is available rather than claim universal deletion.

5. Stop future access

Ask the operator to revoke your bot authorization and stop submitting requests. Server administrators can remove the bot or restrict its channel access. These steps prevent further use or access as configured; they are separate from deletion of existing information.

For the wider data-handling description, read the Privacy Policy. For non-sensitive technical help, see Support.
