A private assistant. Clear ground rules.

Hermes connects an authorized Hermes Cornerstone team member to a privately operated AI assistant through Discord.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Private access only. This is an information site, not a bot installation, account login, or public invitation. Access to the bot is granted separately by the operator.

Which Hermes is this?

The Discord application is named Hermes. Its bot identity at publication is HermesBot#9321. It serves the private Hermes Cornerstone team. The operator is the account holder susheel009; the bot itself is not the legal operator.

Depending on the tools enabled by the operator, Hermes can answer questions, work with files, research topics, and perform authorized actions. It can use the operator’s connected services and computer. Treat access as access to an assistant with real capabilities, not just a chat room.

Before you send a message

Prompts, relevant conversation context, and tool results can be sent to external AI and tool providers. Shared-channel replies are visible to people with access to that channel. Do not send passwords, access tokens, regulated data, or information you lack permission to share.

For the application owner

The policy links above are public so they can be supplied to Discord. They do not certify legal compliance, prove that the bot is online, or confer Discord verification. For a private setup, follow the official Hermes Discord setup guide. Keep access restricted to approved users.
